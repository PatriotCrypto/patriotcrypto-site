A public Bitcoin mining pool I run as a hobby. View live hashrate, active miners and blocks found, and get connection details for your own miner.

The Minuteman BTC

A long-running savings experiment. An automated script buys $10 of Bitcoin each day at noon ET, publishes every purchase, and moves the balance to cold storage for public verification.

theminutemanbtc.com

Blockchain Bible

A free, ad-free King James Bible reader. Each book is inscribed on the Bitcoin blockchain, so readers can independently verify the text.
